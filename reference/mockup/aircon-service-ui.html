2 Your AC System — Where Issues Are Found
Parts A–G — Fixed Reference Template
A
Indoor PCB
Control board inside AHU. Manages fan speed and cooling logic. Failure stops the unit entirely.
B
AHU Fan + Motor + Capacitor
Blower that pushes air through the duct. Worn belt or weak capacitor causes slow airflow or failure.
C
Evaporator Coil
Indoor cooling coil. Removes heat from return air. Grease and dust buildup is the top issue in kitchens.
D
Air Filter
Captures dust before it reaches the coil. A clogged filter cuts airflow and raises energy cost.
E
Filter / Drier
Inline on the liquid refrigerant line. Removes moisture and particles that would damage the compressor.
F
Compressor + Outdoor PCB + Capacitor
Heart of the refrigerant circuit (outdoor). Failure = full shutdown. Highest repair cost of all parts.
G
Condenser Coil + Outdoor Fan Motor
Releases heat outdoors. Dirty coils raise discharge pressure and electricity consumption significantly.
